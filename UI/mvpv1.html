How the room should run

Criteria

Hard (an option that fails one is out)

  • Arrives by 19:30
  • Total within NT$ 6,000
  • At least 5 vegetarian meals

Soft (used to rank)

  • Fewer pickups
  • Shops the teams already like

Done means

  • Every team has confirmed its order
  • Each shop is paid once
  • The courier is booked and tracking is shared

Scope

In: choosing shops, ordering, delivery, payment.
Out: drinks and the venue.

How far the agent may go

On its own

Ask teams, propose options, call votes, record decisions, chase late answers.

Only after the decider approves

Call a shop, book the courier, pay.

Risks

  • Two pickups on one courier order
  • Calling a shop in Mandarin is unproven
  • A team answers late and holds up the order
